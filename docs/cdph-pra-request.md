# Draft: California Public Records Act request to CDPH (SNF changes of ownership)

**Status:** draft, not sent (2026-10-12). Send it only if you decide to; it goes out under your name.

**Why:** AB 1502 (Health and Safety Code §1253.3) requires anyone acquiring a skilled nursing facility in California to apply to CDPH at least 120 days before the change, and to get approval first. The applications are public records, but CDPH doesn't post them (checked 2026-10-12, see `docs/con-feasibility.md`, California). This would be the earliest, most complete California source.

**Where to send:** CDPH's Public Records Act request page, https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Pages/Public-Records-Act-Request.aspx (cdph.ca.gov needs a normal browser; scripted clients fail its TLS check). Records about SNF licensing are held by the Center for Health Care Quality, Centralized Applications Branch (CAB).

**After a response:** if CDPH provides a list (spreadsheet or PDF), it can be loaded the way the Oklahoma and Michigan lists are: facility, applicant (buyer), current licensee (seller), date received, status. A standing monthly request would keep it current.

**Subject:** California Public Records Act request: skilled nursing facility change of ownership and change of management applications (HSC §1253.3)

To the California Department of Public Health, Public Records Act Coordinator:

Under the California Public Records Act (Government Code §7920.000 et seq.), I request copies of the following records held by the Center for Health Care Quality, including its Centralized Applications Branch:

1. A list or log of every application for a change of ownership (CHOW) or change of management (CHOM) of a skilled nursing facility submitted under Health and Safety Code §1253.3 since July 1, 2023, showing for each, where recorded:
   - the facility's name, address and license number (and CMS Certification Number, if recorded);
   - the applicant's name;
   - the current licensee's name;
   - the date the application was received;
   - the application's status, and the date of any approval, denial or withdrawal.
2. If no such list or log exists, the HS 200 (Licensure and Certification Application) form and the CDPH 9167 (Current Licensee Notice – Skilled Nursing Facility Change of Ownership) submitted with each such application.

I'm not requesting any individual's Social Security number, home address, or other personal information exempt from disclosure; please redact such information rather than withhold whole records.

Electronic copies (spreadsheet or PDF) by email are preferred. If any part of this request is denied, please cite the specific exemption and release the remaining portions. If fees would exceed $25, please contact me before proceeding.

These records are requested for a public, non-commercial research database of nursing home ownership changes (https://yutingfan1209.github.io/nursing-home-live-feed/).
